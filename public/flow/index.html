Demand intake — end-to-end process flow

One linear pipeline, four actors, a single decision gate routed by portfolio area. Every action is role-gated: the request always knows who it is waiting on.

1 · Requester 2 · PMO 3 · Technical Team 4 · Area Decisors — Sambini · Gabriela · AI

Rules & guards

The engine blocks dead-ends: every state always has a next action and an owner.

Justification is mandatory

Every Return and every Reject — in triage, evaluation or the decision gate — requires a written justification, stored in the request history.

Capacity before approval

The Technical Team must define delivery team + estimated hours before the request can be sent to the decisor.

Priority before execution

PMO must set the ranking position (score × capacity) before execution can start.

Gate recreated on entry

Whenever a request enters Approval, the decisor gate is recreated as Pending and routed by the current classification — no stale approvals.

Weighted score (100% = 5.00)

Business impact 25 · Risk of not executing 15 · Technical challenge 10 · Revenue 20 · Strategic fit 15 · Stakeholder 10 · Urgency 5. Business impact is automatic from the impact scope.

Notifications

Each hand-off notifies the next actor (inbox + bell badge; Teams/e-mail in the connected version). The requester can always see the current status.
